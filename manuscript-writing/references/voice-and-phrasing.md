## Voice signature

Muhammad Ali Khan writes in a rigorously scaffolded, methods-forward academic register that is instantly recognizable across manuscripts and abstracts. His hallmark is the **funnel**: every piece opens on a broad, field-level pressure (evidence volume outpacing clinicians; a disease's burden; AI outrunning governance) and narrows, paragraph by paragraph, to a single unresolved gap, before pivoting on a fixed hinge — *"To address this gap/these limitations, we…"* / *"we performed / we propose…"* — into a first-person-plural statement of what the work does. He owns his work in the active voice (*we developed, we found, our approach, our analysis clearly indicates*) and slips into agentless passive only for procedures (*Data were abstracted, Patients were identified*). The first-person-plural possessive *our approach / our system / our work*, contrasted against *current approaches* or *existing frameworks*, is a verbal tic that stakes a novelty claim on nearly every page.

His sentences are **long, nominalized, and heavily subordinated** — often 50–80 words stacking appositives, "such as" lists, and semicolon-chained parallel clauses before the verb — and he leans almost mechanically on a fixed set of connectors (*However, Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Notably, Importantly, Taken together*) and on explicit enumeration (*Firstly/Secondly/Thirdly*; *First/Second/Third/Lastly*). He reflexively defines every abbreviation in parentheses on first use and then reuses it relentlessly, frequently coining a compact study nickname (dFLOT, aIO, NAC) or a branded, Title-Case, parenthetically-acronymed framework (LIvE, LISR, LSR, ONCAIR), and he anthropomorphizes system modules with proper names (The Watcher, The Scanner/The Screener, The Extractor, The Analyzer, The Tabulator). These brands recur across papers as a program identity. Note: he is not always internally consistent with a module name within a single paper (the screening module appears as both "The Screener" in the framework list and "The Scanner" in the body/figure legends) — pick one name per manuscript and hold it.

Two temperamental signatures run through everything. **First, constructive humility**: he quarantines limitations into a discrete block and never states a weakness without immediately attaching a mitigation or future-work rebuttal in the same breath (*"However… We aim to…"*), so limitations double as a roadmap; he closes abstracts, discussions, and conclusions on the same cadence — that prospective, multi-site validation is warranted before clinical deployment, and that network-meta-analysis rankings complement but do not replace head-to-head RCTs. **Second, ambition**: he frames work as *first-in-class, next generation, a significant advancement, a paradigm shift from reactive to proactive*, and closes on an expansive, almost manifesto-like vision sentence.

He is a **reproducibility-minded engineer** at heart: he documents provenance obsessively (exact software version numbers, PMIDs, cohort counts, temperature settings, search cutoff dates), tethers every quantitative claim to a specifically numbered exhibit, and translates relative effects into absolute *"N fewer/more events per 1000 patients."* His working drafts are unmistakable — scaffold-first skeletons with ALL-CAPS section headers, big reserved blank blocks, `(X)` citation placeholders, `$XX`/`XX%`/`202X` number placeholders, `IRBXX-XXXX`, inline `NOTE TO ED` annotations, version-stamped filenames, and a light copyedit that lets doubled words and run-together typos survive many versions.

## Sentence and grammar rules

- **Long, subordinated sentences by default.** Stack qualifiers, appositives, and "such as" lists before the verb; chain related items with semicolons rather than splitting into short sentences. (When editing, keep the register but break the genuinely ungrammatical run-ons.)
- **First-person plural, active, for all authorial action** — *we developed, we found, we hypothesized, we conclude with high certainty*. **Never first-person singular.** Switch to agentless passive only for procedures — *Data were abstracted, Patients were identified, Responses were assessed for concordance*.
- **Tense discipline.** Past for what was done and found; present for standing facts, interpretation, and recommendations; present-perfect for contributions (*we have developed, our work demonstrates*); future for proposals (*we aim to, will develop and validate*).
- **Participial-adverbial sentence openers** are a stylistic default in methods/AI writing — *"Utilizing the APIs of GPT and Claude,…", "Leveraging the capabilities of LLMs,…", "Adhering to the token limit,…"*.
- **Explicit enumeration** of arguments, open questions, strengths, and limitations — *Firstly/Secondly/Thirdly/Lastly* or *First/Second/Third*.
- **Deliberate hedging of interpretation**, sometimes stacked — *was potentially the most efficacious, may, might, could suggest, appears to, likely reflect*. State findings and results themselves **flatly, without hedging**; hedge only the interpretation.
- **Define every abbreviation on first use**, then use the short form relentlessly; coin a compact study nickname or Title-Case branded framework where one is warranted.
- **Fixed connector set** to open sentences/paragraphs: *However, Moreover, Furthermore, In addition/Additionally, Notably, Importantly, Interestingly, Conversely, Likewise, Taken together, Of note, It should be noted that.* Use them — but don't run them on autopilot (no three consecutive "However," openers).
- **Topic-sentence-first paragraphs** — state the conclusion, then support it; one idea (or one module) per paragraph.
- **Copyedit slips to fix, never reproduce**: doubled words (*"ATM mutations ATM mutations", "prostate prostate"*), run-together words (*"performednetwork", "withing"*), dropped articles, subject–verb disagreement, double negatives, recurring misspellings (*Certainity → Certainty, continuosly → continuously*).

## Phrase bank

His actual recurring phrases, grouped by function. Use them to sound like him; vary them enough to avoid mechanical repetition.

- **Problem→solution pivots (the hinge):** *"To address this gap," / "To address these limitations," / "To address these challenges," / "To this end," / "To overcome this limitation, we have developed…"*
- **Thesis+roadmap closers for the intro:** *"We performed a systematic review and network meta-analysis to…" / "Here we showcase…" / "Hereby, we outline…"*
- **Novelty claims:** *"To our knowledge, this analysis is the first to…" / "first-in-class" / "a unique approach" / "No automated … currently exists" / "next generation of systematic reviews" / "a significant advancement" / "novel approach."*
- **Templated results verb-frame:** *"X was associated with statistically significant improvement/increase/decrease in [outcome] when compared to Y" / "Mixed treatment comparisons showed that…" / "independently predicted worse [outcome]."*
- **NMA ranking phrase:** *"Ranking analysis showed that … (rank 1; P-score: 0.99) was potentially the most efficacious treatment in terms of …, followed by … (rank 2)."*
- **Standing caveats (to close on):** *"these findings should complement, and not replace, evidence from randomized controlled trials" / "Prospective, multi-site validation is warranted before clinical deployment" / "Validation efforts are required before widespread implementation."*
- **Limitations opener + rebuttal:** *"However, the findings of this study should be interpreted in the context of some limitations."* then *"A potential limitation of our approach is… To address this limitation, we…"*
- **Strengths formula:** *"This study has several strengths. First… Second… Third… Lastly…"*
- **Interpretation pivots:** *"Several observations warrant comment." / "One possible explanation is…" / "Numerous mechanisms have been proposed…" / "Taken together," / "The constellation of these findings suggests…"*
- **Background gap-statement verbs:** *"remain poorly characterized" / "remains unclear" / "little is known about" / "warrants further investigation."*
- **Signature framing tags:** *"patient-important outcomes" / "real-world retrospective cohort" / "shared clinical decision-making" / "most up-to-date and comprehensive evidence" / "as soon as new evidence becomes available" / "living / truly living / in a truly living state."*
- **AI-method identity:** *"collaborative large language models" / "LLMs working in collaboration" / "cross-critique (CC)" / "human-in-the-loop (HITL)" / "zero-shot setting" / "deterministic responses" / "end-to-end automation" / "paradigm shift from reactive to proactive."*
- **Impact vocabulary:** *"streamline" / "enhance the efficiency" / "reduce clinician workload" / "guidelines and guardrails" / "signaling an era characterized by…"*
- **Results anchors:** open Results with *"A total of N…"*; close Results with *"Results are summarized in Table." / "(Table)."*
- **Coordinate doublets/triplets he favors:** *"link, synthesize, and summarize" / "validate, extend, and stress-test" / "created, maintaining, and summarizing."*
